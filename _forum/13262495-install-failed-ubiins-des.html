- id: 67416136 author: "TheM" author_id: 132199639 rank: "Member" date: "2015-10-18 18:20" signature: false body: |-

Finally I got it to work.

Things I did:

After installing nGlide, I copied "glide3x.dll" (32-bit: in "System32"; 64-bit: in "SysWOW64") to "C:\Ubi Soft\Hype - The time quest".

I also set the compatibility mode to "Windows 98/ME", "Run as Admin" and "Run in 640x480 pixels".

Now it finally works! :-)
