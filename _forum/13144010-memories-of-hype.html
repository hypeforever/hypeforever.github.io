- id: 66613528 author: "Findus" author_id: 127714501 rank: "Member" date: "2015-07-09 13:41" signature: false body: |-

Well, I was never able to figure out how to beat Mhasse (Monk). So that room is quite memorable for me, jumping onto his table and trying to figure out, how to deal with that "invincible" bastard.

I actually don't remember how I even got to Mhasse, considering that I was terribly afraid of Granslak, when I was a kid. Meeting a giant clown-ish creature with that constant abhorrent laughing scared the shit out of me. The first glance at him, with that sudden (and way-too-loud) music is therefore pretty memorable. Also I remember feeling overwhelmed by his "invincibility".

Oh and one more thing. That room with the ghosts in the T1 dungeons. When I first replayed that part a couple of years ago on a modern PC, I was so confused why that room seemed so bright - since I remember not being able to see anything in there, aside from the ghosts. Playing that part on an old (tube) monitor seems to be a lot different. Next to me being convinced that the ghosts are "invincible" (sigh) I recall not having any sense of orientation in that room. I must have found the exit out of pure luck, because it was too dark to really see what was going on and I was stressed out because of the flying fellows anyways.

- id: 66614005 author: "Darnok_PL" author_id: 125245904 rank: "Member" date: "2015-07-09 14:44" signature: true body: |-

Well, when I was 5 years old, I was using newspaper, to play (I wanted my parents to read me it ;)).

But when have seen ghosts in Dungeons first I got a bit scared, I run away to Mid Dungeons, saved game and haven't appeared in this room for about 2 years (I have never finished Dungeons IV as a result). In Forest II and Forest III I was just escaping, but in Dungeons it was too scary to try :) .

When I at last won Dungeons IV, my next problem was Barnak. I believe, I was trying to fight with him about 20-30 times, before I managed to beat him (I had 1 mauve potion and some herbs, if I remember well). I managed to win him, when I invented a trick: "it's better to hit him while running backward, so that he won't dissappear". I was 7-8 years old, but I was proud.

My next goal was to find Glacia Ring and Glacia Dragon which were hidden and not explained in newspaper. I was looking for them everywhere (even in forests). I got Dragon quite easy, but for Ring I had to wait many years - I was over 13 years, when I had an idea, to experiment with teleports in Black Tower III (in newspaper I had red-yellow-blue only). I was so happy :D, when I saw the result.

I don't know how many times I have won Hype, I was playing it in Polish, English and German versions (to practise my language), I even played it before oral english-exam and listening helped me a bit :) .

I believe I have won Hype over 250 times in my life (with Hype-Challenge, which is shorter, but more difficult, and it is however something new, so I got not to bored :) ).
