Oh wow, I'm sorry Xdeity, I didn't notice you replied. I put the steps on how to run Hype using Mac OS under another thread. Here are the instructions. Moonlighter, I don't think this is up on the Bugs and Problems section of the website. Would you be able to post this?

Sure, here are the steps

Download Wineskin from http://wineskin.urgesoftware.com/tiki-index.php ;(by the way, this is a free download, and there are no surveys or anything of that sort. this is completely legitement and legal, but not very well known about)

After following installation instructions, open wineskin and click "Create New Blank Wrapper" it will then prompt you to type in a Title of the wrapper, this will be the title of the application.

After Wineskin finishes creating a wrapper, click "View Wrapper in Finder." Right click the file you just created and click "Show Package Contents" and open Wineskin from there.

When Wineskin opens, click "Install Software" and then choose either "Copy a Folder Inside" or "Move a Folder Inside." Choose the installation folder that is available for download on this website.

After installation, go back to the Wineskin menu and choose "Advanced" and be sure that "/UbiSoft/Hype - The Time Quest/MaiD3Dvr_bleu.exe" is designated as the "Windows EXE.

In the "Advanced" menu, click "Tools" and then choose "Winetricks." Open "dlls" and choose the following: "d3dx9_36"; "dinput"; "mfc40"; "mfc42" click "Run" and these essentially drivers will download. (This may take a few minutes)

Finally, close Winetricks and click "Test Run." If it works, it is successfully installed on your Mac/Linux.
