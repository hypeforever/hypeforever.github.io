After some hours and posts on our Facebook group, I was able to figure everything about it out

1.You can do it once per game, it doesn't matter the point you are on

2.Go to Gogoud's Manor I, next to the future Secret Room's Cave exit

3.Type in "DEBUG". A lever will appear for some seconds.
4.Lowering the lever will activate the real debug mode of the game. According to one of the programmers in our Facebook group, it will make some cheats avaliable.

5.The time travel one (which allows leaping through various events across the game) is teleXX, where XX is a number between 00 and 36.

ex.: After activating the lever, tele02 will take you to Fortress I, prior being arrested.

Thank you for pointing this out, this was forgotten it seems...
