We all want to see Hype stay playable, emulation of the PS2 version is very close.

In PCSX2, Hype boots and even upscales in beautiful 1080p or even higher at a full 50FPS.

BUT, there are major graphics problems: half of the ingame graphics are missing and the menu does not show at first. According to the website, changing a setting (EE rounding to Positive) brings up the menus, but the graphics have not been fixed yet.

What may be required to get it playable in the maintime is a CRC hack (a standard hacky fix done for many games), or a cheat (like gameshark code). PCSX2 has debug options built-in. If anyone knows how to troubleshoot this, this might be the area to look! Finally, the next resort would be looking for something in the code itself. Not many of the PCSX2 devs have Hype or are working on it, so someone from this community may be the key!

The fact that it is so close is very encouraging to make this final step! If anyone knows how to help please do!
