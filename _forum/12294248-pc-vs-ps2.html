Hi! I'm new here!* I'm going to play through hype again and i'd like to know what are the differences from the PS2 and PC version and which one is better. I only ever played the pc version and i'd like to try out the PS2 one! Also, is it hard to emulate? I don't have a ps2 anymore...

- id: 60042132 author: "hype1998" author_id: 114877021 rank: "Member" date: "2014-01-27 13:04" signature: true body: |-

They're both (almost) exactly the same game. I own both and the key difference is that the locations are slightly smaller in the PS2 version (The Town of Torras and The Forest are split in two as the Playstation couldn't load the whole area at once). The only other difference is the controls. As there are fewer buttons on the PS2 controller, the unsheathe the sword and unsheathe the crossbow button are the same (you scroll through the weapons rather than picking one immediately) and the sprint button doesn't exist (you push the stick further forward, which is less precise and can be frustrating when having to jump on small platforms (Dungeons, Field of Courage etc.)).

In conclusion, I personally don't think that the PS2 version has anything that the PC version doesn't have. If you already have the PC version then there's no real point in getting the PS2 version.

P.S. In the PS2 version, Gogoud in the era of Taskan II doesn't have a beard. That threw me off a bit.
