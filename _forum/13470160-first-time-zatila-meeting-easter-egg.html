Hi guys!

I noticed a new easter egg in the game and I decided to post it here:

The first time when you meet Zatila in the forest in the era of Taskan I, you can actually attack him with Hype. Just hit him sometimes from the back or the side with your sword BEFORE you talk to him the first time. After hitting him with your sword, talk to Zatila. He will say that he is deeply disappointed that you attacked him and that he thought you were different. I will try to make pictures of it in-game if I have the time! This is really something new I just discovered!

Did you know about this easter egg? Try it out!
