I've recently started doing some attempts at speedrunning Hype: The Time Quest. A lot of my runs have either died from deaths without saving or the game completely crashing so the run I'm linking is the first successful completed one (besides my practice run).

So far my personal best time is 2:54:00:02.

My Run: http://www.twitch.tv/sarick/c/2983766

Apologies that the video quality isn't the greatest, I live in the middle of nowhere in Australia so my internet isn't really capable of much more.

The run itself has a lot of room for improvement, lots of messing up in the jumping sections and even a death late into the run. Some bosses also go horribly like Rajoth (if you beat him without taking damage you get a blue potion and two purple so having a good Rajoth fight helps a lot).

Anyway I figured that the run might interest some of you, and it'd be great to see some more people run the game.

As I see you can fight well against Senekal and Barnak and you don't need 5 mauve potions to cope it (like in other gameplays that I saw on YouTube). I find another challenge interesting too: to win the whole game without taking any potions, herbs, magic nuts, and without visits to blacksmith. Then you cure and charge magic only when you get jewel. Armour is restored in restore-points only (3 in game).

For people interested in hardcore challenges I recommend my mod: http://hypeforever.webs.com/apps/forums/topics/show/12823390-hype-challenge-a-difficult-version-of-hype-for-masters-. It allows to win the game (a bit shortened) without jewels, so Hype's sword doesn't become stronger and therefore you have to hit enemies more that 3 times like normally (in era of Taskan IV it's even 20 times). And Hype's limit of leben doesn't grow up too (if your armour is totally destructed, an enemy in era of Taskan IV kills Hype after 2 hits :/ ).

Hey guys,
I've been improving Sarick's efforts on running Hype.
This is not really news, since my best run is months old and some might have stumbled over it, but I guess it would be nice to mention it on here as well.

So I beat Hype in 2:09:06 - my sum of best segments is 2:05:13 and I am very certain that you could even finish it slightly faster than that. With the strategies I used up to this point I would say it is not possible to go below the 2 hour mark - but then again the huge gap between my PB and SoB shows that I am far from having an 'optimized' run and that there might be more ways to save time that I just don't yet know about.
After getting my current record I did take a break, due to the game soaking up too much time - I did have trouble finding the motivation to go back at running it so far..

What I have found myself motivated to do instead is running the Game Boy Color version that maybe one or two of you know. I'm far from a perfect time, just had a lot of fun playing through it fast without resetting too much. Personal Best is a 32:33 but I am very likely to beat that soon. Did not look for any glitches yet, just exploited some unusual boss behaviours - so no idea how fast it might get. I'm hoping for getting it below 30 minutes.

Anyways, so much for the update.
It's nice to still see people still care about this little game.

EDIT: I totally forgot to mention this. I made a post on the SpeedDemosArchive forums when I started researching / running the game. The information in there is not 100% complete anymore but I'd say it still about 90% of it in there.
