It's not that they're crazy , they're just closed source so people can't re-use the files. Some games nowadays are more open-source or have easily editable files on purpose, so that the users are encouraged to create custom content and mods for the game, and it becomes more popular (eg. The Sims 1, 2 and 3). Games like Hype, on the other hand, are designed to be as closed source as posible, so that no customizations and hacks are done, although they're possible, they're less encouraged.
