Hey Everyone! Good News for our fellow Hype The Time Quest Players! The last release of nGlide (the magical piece of software that allows us to play Hype in modern computers) includes many fixes for glitches we've been experiencing with the 3DFX version. These include:

♦ The fade in\fade out transitions

♦ Wellet on the air after the reaces

♦ Magic spells and other items still on the ground after they've been picked

♦ Some other minor fixes.

To download it, please go to

https://www.zeus-software.com/downloads/nglide

REMEMBER: You must copy the files glide.dll, glide3x.dll and glide2x.dll from C:\Windows\System32 (or Syswow64) into your Hype folder, updating the previous versions of the files inside the folders. Otherwise, the glitches will still happen. Good playing everyone!
