So I've been thinking about Taskan IV's colors, and why they are the same as the armor Hype found in the Field of Courage in Era II.
The game's explanation is thus: Taskan II found the armor in the Lost City and placed it in the Field of Courage for Hype to find. Hype put it on, and traveled to Era III with it. Once in the Fortress of Era III, Taskan III asks Hype to protect his son Taskan IV, who is a young boy at the time. Naturally he looks up to Hype and he comments on Hype's armor, saying that he'd like those colors to be his when he becomes king. And so the legend of the armor has come full circle....

HOWEVER, this doesn't make sense, because in the Intro of the game, we can already see these colors on the Taskan IV's flag. This is impossible, because at that point, Hype hadn't gone back in time yet, never met Taskan IV as a child, and could never have shown him the lost armor.

This seems like a classical time paradox, but if anyone has more insight to this, I'd love to hear their opinion.

I've been thinking about this and I think that the only logical conclusion can be that Taskan IV would have chosen those colours independently had Hype not inspired him in the era of Taskan III.

The fleur de lis in the middle of the crest is the symbol of peace in the game, something which Taskan IV believes in so it is reasonable that he could have chosen it anyway. The chequered blue and white colours could be coincidence.

Another theory is the fact that the Field of Courage is closed in the era of Taskan IV. Due to this, the treasures may have been transferred from it to the fortress, as either King Taskan III or King Taskan IV would have needed money to fund the civil war. Presumably, the lost armour would have been taken with the gold to the fortress, and it was here that Taskan IV could have been able to see it for himself and be inspired by it, as the lost armour is both a symbol of power and peace.

A third theory would be that Taskan II would have recorded information about the armour in an almanac. From here, Taskan IV could have been inspired by the colours from reading the book and learning about the power which the armour holds.
