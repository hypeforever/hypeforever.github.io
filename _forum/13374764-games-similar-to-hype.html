Here we can discuss games similar to Hype (obviously).

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is an obvious Hype-like game. Other Zelda games have similar elements to Hype, but none as much as OoT.

The Hobbit by Sierra games is a great PS2-era platformer/action RPG. It shares a lot in common with Hype; so much so that I would dare to call it the closest we've had to a Hype sequel.

Rayman II: The Great Escape is another platformer by Ubisoft with a very similar tone to Hype. I wouldn't even be surprised if thre were some shared textures or sounds.

Neopets: The Darkest Faerie is another adventure/action/platformer similar to Hype and the Hobbit. I've never played this game, but I saw a streamer playing it on Twitch and was amazed by how similar the graphics and gameplay looked.

Lastly, the Souls series of games has some similarities (obviously both take place in a medievel fantasy setting and involve exploring dungeons and abusing potions :D) and is another series I regret to have never played (someday...)

Any other games? Let me have em!
