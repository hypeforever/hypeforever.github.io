i'm working in a written adaptation of hype (I've written the first chapter) and I've already thought about some of the things of the plot.

it will be narrated in 3rd person, mostly showing hype and gogoud's point of view, but also adding some chapters with others points of view (like nolhim, vibe, rave, enost, lzyothe and maybe senekal and wellet)

I agree with the idea (like the user salem vendari, who tried to do another adaptation project) of hype being helped by other characters e.g.:

  • talboth and kaltran helping him to escape from the dungeons
  • the wizards of the laboratory fighting with him against rajoth
  • nolhim stealing the almanacs with him
  • showing how vibe leads tasckan IV's army against barnak's army

I will separate the story in four books. one for each era

i will use part of the theory of the user dirty_hype( https://hypeforever.github.io/forum/topics/12826713-hype-paradox/ ) about gogoud and hype origin

i would like to make the battles more than a strength and endurance matter. I would like to add smart strategy and the feels

also, each era will be asociated with a season(spring in t1, summer in t2, autumn in t3 and winter in t4)

I will give relevance to each spell of the game

unluckily I will write this in spanish, i would be glad if someone help me with the translation

if someone have any theory that is worth be explained in this story, or any interesting backstory for any character, or just anything that could make this a better story, please comment it
