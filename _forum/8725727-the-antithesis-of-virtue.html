Hmmm I'm not quite sure I understood this. From my point of view (and again, this game is far too emotional and complicated to be a child's game - and much more complex than Zelda), to obtain the Jewel of Virtue, Hype must confront himself in battle - his dark side, that is truly just a part of him. By defeating the part of him that is the "anthithesis of virtue", all that is bad and evil inside of him, he becomes pure. He becomes Virtue himself. A saint. Probably, the only one among man who is worthy of the Jewel of Virtue, the only among man who could erradicate any form of evil from his soul. This explains the "why" DH appears part: Hype had to prove to the gods he was 100% pure and virtuous, by defeating Dark Hype and then being worthy of the Jewel.
