Hype The Time Quest is an emotional rollercoaster - as said one review I read this week. You go through 4 different eras, and in this period you meet people, you become friends with them and some of them die. What do you think are the saddest moments in the game?

In my opinion, the goodbye to Gogoud in Taskan III is the worst one. You suddenly realizes the man worked all his life only for you, while he could have been a brilliant magician, invent stuff etc...and when you say goodbye, you are sure he won't be there for you in Taskan IV. It's just so sad...of course, everything about Taskan IV era is depressing, but still.....
