- id: 70142082 author: "Chuckchuk" author_id: 85643792 rank: "Member" date: "2018-01-31 20:09" signature: false body: |-

Hey! I was just thinking of how great and manageable it would be to make a Hype reimaginging, when I remembered about HypeForever! Well I saw the facebook page, and it's awesome to see that there's actual progress on a Hype remake! I've several ideas myself, and I can do some modelling, just no texturing really. I'm awful at texturing.... I think I could make pretty good landscapes.

Another thing, I've been thinking of how the game could be re-imagined. For one, the map could have a few things added to it, we could have a longer stretch of area between Gogoud's manor and Torras. We could add entire new areas, loads of new NPC's, you could have NPC's coming in/out of the houses of Torras and walking around town, have it feel more alive. Another thing, we could have Torras actually be a lot bigger too. I know that you guys have already started working on Torras, and it looks great so far, but I seriously think it'd be a good opportunity to put the community's touch on the game, and add some things that were originally intended in the concept art that never took fruition. For example, the blacksmith was supposed to have a way bigger set-up if you look at some of the concept art: https://hype.tips/application/files/4614/3267/3784/blacksmith.jpg

Another thing I was thinking about was perhaps the combat. I really found the combat hard as a kid, and even now I can see that the enemies move really fluidly, but since the fan-base has grown up, why don't we add some interesting and somewhat challenging combat?

I remember that the old armor system used to be that if you took too much damage, your defense would weaken until you went and fixed your armor. Well! We could add things like a block button to let hype block, and if you block then you may loose some shield durability which you have to fix later, so that you can't turtle for example. We could also make dodging more of a mechanic, and could have some more sword play than just the one swing. I don't know, I have several ideas in my head for the combat. I wouldn't want to change it THAT much, since it was part of the beauty of the original, but it might still be a decent idea to complicate the combat a little, to make it more of a challenge. But there's so many awesome aspects that could be expanded upon, such as the really cool magic, the crossbow, and the sword-play.

Then of course we could also better the AI slightly, and add more enemies about so that it'd be harder to get around at times, maybe forcing you to sneak around sometimes (without adding a silly sneak mode).

I don't know, I've so many ideas, and I can model as well, (Though as stated, can't texture) I'd really love to give input and perhaps help with the Hype Project.
