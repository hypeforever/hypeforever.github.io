Open Broadcaster (OBS) is good if you want 720p60 (or 1080p60 if you have good enough PC, or any other resolution or fps you want) on Youtube, but you'll get 16:9 aspect ratio (I think, that's what happens to me), which you might not like since Hype is playing in 4:3. But personally I would rather do that since Open Broadcaster makes really clean video. Open Broadcaster is probably the best free recording software on Windows in general.

Link: https://obsproject.com/

If you decide to use that, here are the settings I would recomend for 720p 60fps on Hype:

  • Max bitrate (kb/s): should be 6000-8000 or above to get a clean video on 720p60fps on Hype The Time Quest (Most important step cuz too low will make the video look really bad), higher is better, but the video file will get bigger too.
  • Broadcast Settings should be File Output Only, but since you're not broadcasting, I don't know if it's nessacary to change, but I did it just in case.
  • Choose File Path to customize where the videos gets saved.
  • Set Base Resolution to the same as your Monitor Size, and Resolution Downscale to 1280x720, Filter: should be best Quality, Fps: 60.
  • Most other Settings could be Default I think, unless you know what you're doing.

If your computer isn't powerful enough with these settings (using too much CPU) you could try switching Encoders. x264 is default, but you could try NVIDA NVENC or Quick Sync.

That's all tips I have, you can use other Settings, but these are just what found out was best for me, Hope this helps :)

Remember, these are just what I recomend for Hype The Time Quest. If you would like to record the screen for other stuff like browsing the web I would recomend 1080p 30fps, and lower bitrate I guess.
