Silver is an awesome game from Infogrames (Now Atari). Released in 1999, SILVER takes you through the land of Jarrah, where the evil emperror Silver captures all the ladies in procreating age, in order to find himself a new wife. You control David and his group, as he embarks on a quest to save his lovely wife, Jennifer.
