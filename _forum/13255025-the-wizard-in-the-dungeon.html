I'm always struck by the wizard (I think his name was Daniel...?) in the first era dungeon. Although his main purpose is to give you a hint about the tri-blast ice magic, his exchange with Hype has some interesting and subtle charisma to it. It's one of the only moments in the game where you are free to make your own ethical choice--usually decisions to help someone are required for progress through the game. But you're free to continue out of the dungeon without freeing the wizard, whom (a bit disappointingly) you'll never see again either way. I suppose it's a bit of a testimony to Hype's power that a small gesture he makes in time can free someone or leave them rotting in a small cell for the rest of their life. I'm not even able to fully grasp why I find this moment powerful. Any others have thoughts on this?
