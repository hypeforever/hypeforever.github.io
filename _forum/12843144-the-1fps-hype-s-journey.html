The Unknown Landscape 2

Some time, someway I randomly managed to reach a place that Darnok_PL has called Unknown Landscape 2 (by jumping in the “sea” in front of Gogoud’s manor):

https://hypeforever.github.io/photos/

My guess on how it did happen was: “my vertical speed was so high that I someway clipped through the respawn trigger area”. I also guessed that the lower the fps, the higher the chance I clip through.

Walls

Before going further, let me define the two kind of walls there are in the game.

Let’s say the standard jump height is H.

  • The straight walls: when you jump against it, you’ll jump below H
  • The curved walls: when you jump against it, you’ll stand in the air a bit longer than the usual and you’ll jump slightly above H.

(Sometimes, walls are not graphically straight/curved but physically are)

Running in low fps

Today, I first ran seven times dolphin (the gamecube/wii emulator) which is quite heavy and then I ran Hype. The game was around 1fps.

In this 1fps world, the physical laws were slightly different from the normal world :

  • On landing after a jump, 50% of the time Hype was doing its hurt-by-high-distance-fall noise.
  • Sometimes the camera would stop following me, sometimes it’d be in late following me.
  • Being in 1fps neither did make reaching the Unknown Landscape 2 easier nor helped clipping through triggers.
  • In 1fps, jumping against a curved wall makes you jump WAY above H (super jump). Actually, the lower the fps the higher the jump. Also hitting those walls in the air would sometimes give you a strange horizontal inertie. Here are some examples:

https://youtu.be/7oUQT04qbOw

https://youtu.be/JLrmSXtJfX8

https://youtu.be/3x_6bTF-gDs

  • Every single time I went out of bounds (thanks to super jump) I ended falling for a while, in the black emptiness, then game over. We can notice I didn’t respawn as it usually happens when you fall in the water.

My conclusion

Reaching the Unknown Landscape 2 is NOT due to clipping through the respawn trigger, I’m convinced otherwise having a low fps would help.

My new theory

Respawn triggers have been relevantly put in some specific areas by the developers. But they made some mistakes, indeed, some areas have not been fully covered of triggers. Thus, there are holes in the sea of Gogoud. If you fall in one of those holes (might be extremely hard to perform), you usually fall in a black space for a while and then game over. BUT for some reasons, there are some island of safeness in this black world that stands waaaaaaay below the normal world. They are the Unknown Landscapes. So, if you manage to go through a trigger-hole, you may then have a chance to land on it (otherwise it’s game over).

On an island

If you manage to reach an Unknown Landscape, well, first congratulation. Then, physics is slightly weird in there, feel free to test it. From what I know, you have no risk of dying if you stay on this island. And it seems like you don’t die directly when leaving it.
