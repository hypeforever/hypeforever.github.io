I thougt I'd say hi after joining this forum. When I first played hype, it was after a year of anticipation. I was never much of a lego kid, but I loved playmobil, especially the knights. So when I heard of this video game I wanted to play it so badly. It took me a while, and by chance I got my hands on the last copy in the store (back when they were the place to go to for pc games, and even back then Hype was undervalued, got it for the equivalence of 2 dollars today).

The game is still on my shelve now, roughly 15 years later. What can I say? The game is amazing. I host a gaming group, and whenever the hype soundtrack comes on (which it does frequently) it fits like a glove.

A while back (which is 4 years...darn) I started a let's play of Hype. I only got to part 4 (you can still find them by youtubing: Endarmir hype), but my life got in the way, and when I got back I was so dissatisfied with the videos that I didn't feel like picking it up again. I already noticed that one of the loyal watchers back then is active on this forum. My regards from four year earlier sir (or madam)!

I should probably include my proudest moments in the game. Three come to mind really quickly:

- The brigrands' Village. Love the music, the atmosphere, the people.

- When I got higher on gogoud's tower by using the broken chest there. It took me somewhat like thirty minutes. I don't know if I would have persisted now, but I sure did back then.

- Gogoud no longer being there in era 4. The music in the tower and his missing presence... probably the saddest I ever was during a video game (then I found Nohlin...)

I could go on and on about my great memories, but I'll leave it at that. So yeah, that is about it. If you have any questions or feel like reminiscing with me, feel free to do so!
