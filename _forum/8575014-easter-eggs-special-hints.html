Easter Eggs

*Next to the entrance of Laboratories II, there is a girl standing over some barrels. If you blow them up, she'll be sent into deep space. If you visit this area during Taskan IV, she falls from the sky and runs away

*References to Rayman, Speed Busters and the Tower of Pizza, in Italy, can be found on the Champion's Room (IV)

*There is a strange sign with the name "Tania" written on it in the room where you meet Gogoud (Champion's Room). It remains unknown what is the meaning of the name, or what does it refeers to.

*There is an image of Barnak in one of the windows in "Lost City".

*Terryo, (Nohlin's Father), can be found, on your second visit to Bringand Village II,next to the future entrance of the room where you'll find the Electric Magic: Javelin. in Taskan III.

*Rajoth grows from a little boy to an old man in just one era. The girl you send to space takes 2 eras to become an adult woman. Weird.

Special Hints

*The first Mauve potion can be obtained by striking Granslak's Hat after defeating him.

*If you keep talking to the woman outside The Inn in Taskan II era, she'll end up dropping some money.

*Killing All Bats in The Forest\In The Manoir and Killing all Soldiers in the Fortress gives you rewards.

*You can actually fight the Gladiator in Taskan II - Field of Courage as many times as you want. He will always be there.
