Granslak:
After you hit Granslak a few times, he will lose balance for a moment. If you jump into him, he will be defeated immediatly.
Mhasse:
A good way to make him fall down is to have a position where he will come and be between you and the place where he must go to fall.
Rajoth:
Using Ice magic against him is effective, giving the fact that he is a Fire-Mage.
The Gladiator:
A good way to defeat him is to follow him very close on every jump and when you hit him, go back a little so he doesn't hit you back.
The Three Headed Dragon:
When there's only 1 head in screen, stay in the same place and turn around when you have to hit him.
Senekal:
When he throws his Hammer, you can use magic, so it doesn't make any damage. Works with other bosses too.
The antithesis of virtue:
Moving from left to right with A and S when he hits with his sword, moving with A or S (Only with one) When he shoots arrows (Or shoot back) and go back when he starts levitating to use magic, given the fact that it doesn't have so much range. It's also helpful to shoot arrows when moving with A and S.
Armored-Enost:
Hiting him with the sword while moving with A and S and running around the room when he starts attacking you with his cape.
