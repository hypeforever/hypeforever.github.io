Hey, I believe HTTQ is quite to easy for people, who have played this game many times, so I have created mod, which by swap of maps creates a very hard version of Hype withouth jewels, which in spite of it allows to win the whole story. It's only a bit shortened. Therefore stregth of Hype's sword and his limit of life and magic energy don't grow up, and enemies are everytime harder to defeat (in era of Taskan IV you have to hit a typical soldier 20 times!).

Instead of sundial, to travel in time you will use following portals/byways:

To the future:

Monastery I -> Monastery II

Brigand Village II -> Brigand Village III

Lost City III -> Gogoud's Manor IV

Lost City IV -> Black Tower IV

To the past:

Gogoud's Manor IV -> Gogoud's Manor III

Gogoud's Manor III -> Gogoud's Manor II

Gogoud's Manor II -> Gogoud's Manor I

In one era:

Brigand Village III -> Gogoud's Manor III (it's the only way to visit Gogoud in era of Taskan III)

Detailed information in file Readme.txt (in zip-file), please read it, if somebody wants to try my version, it includes very important things.

Link: https://speedy.sh/nu6aB/Hype-Challenge.zip

It works on every language version of Hype. I have just tested and won it.

I am waiting for comments and opinions.

Well, I have written all required information in Readme. To install Challenge, you have to put "Hype-Challenge.bat" in main installation folder "Hype - the time Quest" and to activate my file ONCE. It just renames level's files of game.

I believe I will make a better installator later, when I get more knowledge on programming.

If you do this right, game will still work and you start your mission on Gogoud's Manoir I. Some levels will be swapped and for example while getting into Monastery I you will appear in Monastery II - it will be the only way to get to era II, because you won't get any jewels here. Therefore game will be more difficult.

If you want to return to normal Hype you can run bat-file "Restore-Hype" that repairs all swapped files.
