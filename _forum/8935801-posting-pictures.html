Greetings :)

Welcome to Hype The Time Quest forever. This is a place where all Hype fans can discuss the game and everything related to it.

Currently, we're hosted on webs.com. Webs is a great website hosting service, and

I'm overall satisfied with webs, it gives much more freedom than other hosting services, so that I can manage and edit the site as I do.

However, it has its own limitations. One of them is related to pics. Regular members

(and moderators as well) are unable to upload pictures to the forum sections of the site. To be able to post pictures along with your messages, you are required to create an account in "photobucket.com"

Then, when you want to add an image, just press the image button and the link from the image in photobucket

ex: the link is
