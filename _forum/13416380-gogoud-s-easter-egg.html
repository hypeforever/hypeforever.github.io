I have randomly got a very weird easter egg that I've called Gogoud's Easter Egg:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2PnMMmb3EjQ

It was discovered by accident, by random messing around - that was ridiculously lucky to happen and it was also not easy to guess the key combination.

If you press some specific key combination in Gogoud's Manoir I, it causes many interesting levels. It allows you to enter Black Tower IV at the beginning.

Because it's working Gogoud's Manoir I level only, it seems to be a cool easter egg rather than a cheat code.

It has also apparently happened by accident to MothBallFamicom in one of his lives:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TXPK8_40UK0
