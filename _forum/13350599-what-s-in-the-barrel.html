In Taskan II, outside the laboratories, is a young girl standing on an explosive barrel. Obviously the gentlemanly thing to do would be to leave her alone. Despite this, we all seem to take the immoral action of deciding that this girl would be much happier in space. She leaves in Taskan II as a girl and returns fully grown in Taskan IV. So, what would we need in the barrel for such a thing to occur?

The first thing that we have to consider is escape velocity. A typical child’s weight is about 30kg and escape velocity is 11.19km/s. As she goes straight up vertically, we can assume that she will not go into orbit, and any speed less than escape velocity would mean that she would almost certainly come back down well before Taskan IV. Using some of the most conservative numbers that I could find, I calculated that at the very least, you would probably need about 4GJ of energy to accomplish this, equivalent to almost a metric tonne of TNT, or about as much energy as you need to heat a medium sized house for a month.

However, she does not come back as an old woman, but rather a middle aged woman. Taking Wellet as a reference, one era is enough to go from a child to an adult, or an adult to an old person. This girl aged one eras-worth in two eras. This is where time dilation comes in. In order for this to happen, she must have been travelling at about 0.866c, or 260,000km/s. This gives us the staggering value of needing about 1,000,000,000,000,000,000J of energy (1EJ) at minimum to achieve this. To put this into perspective, this speed is about double the speed of light in diamond; and this energy is about the amount required to supply the entire United States for 1 month. In terms on TNT, this is equivalent to about 239,000,000,000kg, or half the mass of every single human on Earth.

These speeds take their toll in other ways too. XKCD did a very similar calculation on this and the effects would be practically identical. The girl would be travelling so quickly that atoms from the air would fuse to her body, releasing lethal quantities of gamma rays. Hype and the people of Torras would certainly be disintegrated. In addition to this, relativity means that her height would be roughly halved and her mass would be roughly doubled as she travels.

If she went into an orbit around the sun at this speed, she would be able to orbit the sun, come back to Earth, and repeat about 3 million times before returning in the era of Taskan IV. However, the fact that she came back at all indicates the presence of something about 66 light years from Earth with a huge mass, a black hole. She would have to use the gravity boost to complete a half turn and return in exactly the same direction which she came from.

Ignoring all practical concerns, and how improbable it is that she would return to exactly the same spot on Earth (this would be by far the least likely thing to have ever happened ever) this theory would explain it. But back to the original question, what’s in the barrel? The largest nuclear weapon ever detonated was the Tsar Bomb in 1961. It was so large that it could give incredibly severe third degree burns to people 70km away as soon as the fireball hit them, (although you would feel no pain as it would fry your nerves). The barrel must contain about 4 Tsar Bombs. This is also equivalent to the entire amount of energy from the sun that hits the Earth in 6 seconds. Remember, this calculation was for the minimum amount of energy and it assumes that the entirety of this energy is transferred only to the girl in the vertical direction. The true value would be much, much larger than that.

I may leave her alone next time.
