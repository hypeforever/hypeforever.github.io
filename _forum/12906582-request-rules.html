Hello Everyone!
Welcome to our new section, Requests.

For many years, I have taken requests, in order to help people that come to the Hype website. I have always done so and I'm happy to help, as many of you know it. My objective is to help as many ppl as possible to play Hype, and I will keep on doing it.

I've decided to create this section so that I could keep track of everything that you guys ask me to do. I always have that feeling that I forgot to do something, and this will help me not to. However, I've decided to estabilish some small "rules" for taking requests, so that my job is at least worthwhile:

-The requester must have at least 5 posts.

-The requester must have a profile pic (any pic other than the default one)

Keep in mind that these rules applies only to requests that I will be taking. Other members are, of course, free to take requests and solve problems by themselves :)

(*Requests refer to anything except for written help, which I'll always do anyway).
