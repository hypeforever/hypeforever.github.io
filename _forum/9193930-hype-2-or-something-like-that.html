Hey!

The problem is Hype didn't go as expected. It was marketed - and they sincerely hoped it to be - the PC version of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. As you may notice, however, it didn't go this way. Of course, Hype has its fans around the world but the question is: Was it able to form a fanbase just as solid as Zelda? The obvious answer is no. It didn't sell well, it didn't give the feedback they expected.And this is the reason why Ubisoft never made another Hype, and probably never will.
