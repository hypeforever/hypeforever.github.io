Although this game hasn't been released yet and very little is known about it, it is a forthcoming game from Panache Digital Games - a company started by many former Ubisoft Montréal employees. They describe the project as a third-person action/adventure survival game, which sounds promising.

The project is being lead by Patrice Désilets, the man who created Assassin's Creed and worked on Prince of Persia: The Sands of Time (arguably two of the greatest time travel games of all time).

More importantly however, he worked on Hype: The Time Quest (definitively the greatest time travel game of all time).

What is perhaps more exciting is that the senior game designer and scriptwriter is Philippe Debay, who wrote Hype! In his bio on the website, he states that Hype: The Time Quest is his favourite game.

Ancestors: The Humankind Odyssey is far from completion, I believe that it is in pre-Alpha at the moment - so don't expect anything until at least 2019 - but I thought that it was exciting that the project heavily involved these two people, not to mention many other programmers and designers who worked on Hype as well.
