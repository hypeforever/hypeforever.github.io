## Gogoud's Manor I Cheat Code

This is a relatively recent discovery of our member
[Darnok_PL]({{ "/forum/members/125245904-darnok-pl/" | relative_url }}).

Apparently not listed anywhere over the internet, it seems programmers left
something behind meant to be used for testing purposes...or not, it's unknown.
Thing is, pressing CTRL+SHIFT+DEL anytime during the game will have some
interesting effects:

- You will be able to travel through all eras
- Your sword will change to the updated charged sword
- If you visit Zatilla at Forest IV, and climb on his back, you'll spawn at the
  sky, exactly at the black tower entrance spot

If you do this cheat at the very beginning of the game, during the first
dialogue (you can do it as soon as you get up from the ground after being
brought back to life by Gogoud), a special transform will occur, changing your
armor and sword! You'll also be able to strike the sundial, even though the time
portals won't be visible; standing in the symbol of the desired era and striking
it twice will take you to that era. The gate to Gogoud's Manor will also be
open, even though you won't have the key yet.

the exit of the cave. If you're fast enough to activate it, you'll be in debug
mode. Some effects are still unconfirmed, but among other things it allows you

## Hints for Hype

{% comment %}Restored from the 2017 version, it was lost in later versions of the webs.com page.{% endcomment %}

1. It's Always cheaper reforcing the armor in Torras I, and it's the same fix.
2. It's Always chepaer buying things in a previous era.
3. There ARE 10 behives in Forest 3, but there is one that is very-well hidden.
4. Run the races of Wellet and Jamous as soon as you can. You won't need a 10
   blue potion upgrade in Taskan IV, you'll need a 10 MAUVE POTION!
5. Killing all bats in a area (except for the sewer) always gives you something
   at the first time you do it.

### Where\How do you obtain:

**(10) Blue Potion Upgrade**
: Run and win Jamous race in Torras (Taskan I). He can be found with some
  chickens at the town.

**(10) Red Potion Upgrade**
: Run and win the Wallet race on the Forest (Taskan 1). She can be found on a
  hill, in the forest, near the gates.

**(10) Mauve Potion Upgrade**
: The only automatic upgrade in the game. Young\Prince Taskan III gives this to
  you after the "Weapons From The Past" Mission.

**(10) Green Herbs Upgrade**
: For this one, destroy 10 beehives in the Forest (Taskan I). It will spawn on
  the round-shaped hill in the forest.

**(10) Yellow Herbs Upgrade**
: Destroy the 10 Beehives in the Forrest (Taskan III, no easy task finding the
  last one!)

**(50) Blue Crossbow Bolts Upgrade**
: For This one to appear, you must destroy the 10 behives in Torras (Taskan I).
  It will spawn on the roof of a building next to the Torras Gates.

**(50) Red Crossbow Bolts Upgrade**
: For This one to appear, you must destroy the 10 behives in Torras (Taskan II).
  It will spawn next to Wallet, outside the Fortress Gates.

**(500) Plastiks Upgrade**
: For this one, you must run and win the Wellet Race in Torras. He can be found
  outside the Fortress gates (Taskan II).

**The Running Boots**
: Meet a very old Wellet outside Torras Gates, in the forrest (Taskan III).
  He'll ask you to race the classic race again (Ah, What memories). If you win,
  he will give you no more no less than the Running Boots.
  *Note: For this event to trigger, you need to complete the Senekal Weapons
  Mission.*

## Trivia

Here you'll find info on lesser-known facts of the game, and also easter-eggs
and as such.

- During the Era of Taskan IV, right before meeting Gogoud's Ghost in the
  Fortress, you can see an image of Rayman on the bed, and there's also a
  picture of Hype next to the Pisa Tower, in Italy.
- Sometime before heading to the Black Tower (The Search for The Flags),
  Taskan III can be found on the Fortress III again. He'll say he's sad and
  getting tired. Also, he will tell you that the flag has been stolen...
- You can send the little girl, who stands over some barrels next to the
  Laboratories 2, into space, by shooting an arrow at the barrels. If you do so,
  and visit the same area in the era of Taskan IV, you'll see her falling from
  space, as a grown woman.
- While the girl above took 2 Eras (100 years?) to grow into an adult, Rajoth
  grew from a little child into an old man in just one era.
- You can acquire a Mauve potion before you even find a Red one - If you strike
  Granslak's cap\hat after defeating him in the Fortress, one potion spawns in
  the center of the room.
- Taskan II is the only era where you don't visit the Fortress - nor by the
  roofs, nor by the sewer. Taskan II is also the only king you never meet.
- Gogoud and Zatila are the only characters present in all eras, besides Hype,
  of course.
- Terryo can be found in Bringand Village 2, next to the entrance of the room
  where you find the electric magic in Taskan III. He's the leader of
  bringands. You can only meet him if you go to the village right after
  finishing the Field of Courage level. He'll be surprised you didn't steal
  anything from the treasure room and will use you as a role model for future
  genearations of brigands.
- The name "Taskan" comes from "Alain *Tascan*", the director of the game.
- Have you ever noticed these characters' names? "Hype, Rave, Vibe"? And how
  they are all related? (Rave is the champion right before Hype, who marries
  Vibe)
